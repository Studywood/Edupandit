Emotion Voice Studio

Write a line, start it with a bracket naming the emotion. Every line can have its own emotion and accent. One female voice, one fixed pitch.

Format: [emotion] or [emotion+emotion, accent] then the text. Lines without a bracket use neutral. Click a tag to insert it at the cursor.
Voice to voice

Wear headphones so the mic doesn’t pick up the speaker. If the mic is blocked, open this page in its own Chrome or Edge tab.

Mode 1: speak, get the clean female voice

Your words are recognised, then spoken by the locked female voice with the emotion you choose. Same voice every time.

Mode 2: live voice changer

Your own voice and timing, raised in pitch and brightened to sound feminine.

Lines

Works offline using your device’s built-in voices. Emotion is created with speed, volume, pauses and breathy fillers, because browser voices cannot truly act. Pitch is never touched, so the voice stays identical. A different accent means a different built-in voice, so it will sound like a different person; leave the accent on one setting if you need the exact same voice everywhere.
